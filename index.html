How I work

From the first idea.
To the final cut.

A clear process, from our first conversation to the final delivery.

  1. 01

    Brief & direction

    We discuss your goals, references, available footage and deadlines, then agree on the creative direction and deliverables.

  2. 02

    Create & edit

    I build the first cut, shaping the story, pacing and sound. Motion graphics and AI-generated visuals are added where they support the project.

  3. 03

    Review & refine

    You review the first version and share your feedback. I refine the edit to bring it in line with the agreed direction.

  4. 04

    Final delivery

    Once the edit is approved, I prepare and deliver the final files in the agreed formats.
